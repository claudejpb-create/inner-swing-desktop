Inner Swing — Resonate with your body's rhythm

Find Your Inner Swing Rhythm

Everyone's baroreflex has its own natural rhythm: the breathing pace at which heart rate rises and falls the most. This is your resonance pace, your Inner Swing rhythm.

The swing will guide you through 7 paces, from 6.5 down to 3.5 breaths per minute. Just breathe with it. At each pace the page measures how strongly your heart rate rises and falls, and then suggests the pace where it was strongest.

  1. Put on your strap, wet the electrodes and connect it.
  2. Sit comfortably, feet on the floor, shoulders relaxed.
  3. Breathe gently with the swing: in as it sways one way, out as it goes the other. Don't force deep breaths.
